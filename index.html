Version 2
  1. A. Interactive map prototype

    Status: Current

About this version

Starts from version 1, with the client’s feedback applied.

Changes

Community nodes
  • Community node changed to dot
  • High content nodes are larger bush green dots, with a 2px green outline, selected pin and label, and groups holding one turn green
  • Filtered-out nodes now disappear from the map
Map labels and zoom
  • All caps text changed to sentence case
  • Place names show two zoom levels earlier
  • Region labels enlarged to 16px, in dark red
  • Region labels move out of the way of nodes and place names
  • Smoother zoom animation, with place names held still until it stops
Community profiles
  • Region and service provider details moved under the place name; the sand band now holds only works
  • Service provider now shows the regional provider for each community, in full
  • Traditional Owners and languages added to low content profiles
  • Profile details aligned across high and low content; population added underneath
  • “Works” heading removed from profiles
  • Photos are a simple gallery, with no “view more”
Filters
  • Service provider filter switched on
  • Filter groups fold up, and open on a click
  • Filter options indented, restyled to match the works, and open groups highlighted
